# Product Teardowns

Three short teardowns of products I have used for more than eight years: **bKash**, **Pathao** and **foodpanda**. Each one covers who the product serves, how it earns, where it stands against rivals, what I have noticed as a long-time user, and two changes I would make, with a test plan and the metrics I would watch.

The facts come from public reporting, and every source is listed at the end of each PDF. The observations and recommendations are my own.

## Read them

| Product | What it covers | Main idea | PDF |
|---|---|---|---|
| bKash | Mobile money, trust and security | Make fingerprint or face confirmation the default for every transaction, and show people where their complaint stands | [bkash-teardown.pdf](bkash-teardown.pdf) |
| Pathao | Ride-hailing super app and payments | Make discounted rides fair for riders and safe for passengers, and make topping up Pathao Pay easier | [pathao-teardown.pdf](pathao-teardown.pdf) |
| foodpanda | Food delivery marketplace | Add a quick in-app order confirmation, and give restaurants a clear view of what they earn per order | [foodpanda-teardown.pdf](foodpanda-teardown.pdf) |

Each teardown is about three pages.

## How each teardown is structured

1. What it is
2. Who uses it and why
3. How it earns
4. Against the competition
5. What I have noticed as a long-time user
6. What works and what doesn't
7. What I would change, with a test plan and risks
8. How I would measure it
9. Sources

## About the approach

- I wrote the observations from my own use of each app. They come from a small number of experiences, so I treat them as starting points, not proof of a general pattern.
- Where public figures disagree or come from opinion pieces, the teardown says so.
- The recommendations are ideas, not company plans, and I have not tested them with real users.
